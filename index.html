Why Does the Car Fly Off Crests?
On a convex hill crest of curvature κ = 1/R, centripetal force requires mg cosθ − N = mv²/R. When speed v is high enough that v²/R ≥ g cosθ, the normal force drops to N = 0 and the car enters free parabolic flight!

1. Mechanical Work Formula:

W = F · d · cos(θ)

= 50 × 10 × cos(30°)

2. Mechanical Power Formula:

P = W / t

= 433.01 / 4.0

= 108.25 Watts

Sign of Work:
• 0° ≤ θ < 90°: Positive Work (speeds object up)
• θ = 90°: Zero Work (force perpendicular to motion)
• 90° < θ ≤ 180°: Negative Work (opposes motion, e.g., friction)
